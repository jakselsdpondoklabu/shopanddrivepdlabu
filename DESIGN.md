# Design Direction: Super Shop&Drive Cabang Ampera

## 1. Identity & Character
- **Brand:** Super Shop&Drive by Astra Otoparts
- **Branch:** Cabang Ampera Raya, Jakarta Selatan
- **Tagline:** The Trusted Parts Shop
- **Core Aesthetic:** Precision Automotive Workshop, High-Trust, Authoritative, Clean & Efficient
- **Design Inspiration:** Google Stitch AI Design System (`Stitch AI Design System` / `Precision Auto Workshop`)

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary / Action Red (`#E3000F`):** Dedicated to high-priority conversions, key CTA buttons ("Booking via WhatsApp", "Cek Antrean"), promo tags, and urgent state actions.
- **Secondary / Automotive Navy (`#002884`):** Anchors navigation headers, prominent card headlines, primary titles, and corporate framing.
- **Tertiary / WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`):** Reserved exclusively for live communication, instant messaging hooks, and the persistent floating contact button.
- **Surface Dark (`#0B132B`) & Surface Dark Elevated (`#1C2541`):** Cinematic contrast for Hero backdrop scrim, dark Testimonials section, and global footer.
- **Promo Amber (`#F59E0B`):** 5-star rating stars, highlight discount badges, and urgency notices.
- **Canvas Light (`#F8FAFC`) & Border Subtle (`#E2E8F0`):** Clean workshop background and crisp hairline separators.
- **Text:** `#0B1C30` (headline dark), `#64748B` (slate body & metadata).

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif
- **Headlines:** Bold / Extra Bold (700 / 800) for authoritative mechanical clarity.
- **Body & Data:** 400 with generous line-height for quick scanning of service specs and checklists.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Medium to Bold (600 - 700) with slight letter-spacing for status badges and table headers.

## 4. Dials & Antislop Principles
- **ENERGY 2 (Balanced):** Dynamic automotive colors, vibrant red CTA, clean white-slate containment.
- **RHYTHM 2 (Varied Narrative Cadence):** Dark Hero -> Light Service Grid -> Promo Spotlight -> Dark Testimonial Wall -> Maps & Contact -> Dark Footer.
- **MOTION 1 (Purposeful Hover & Pulses):** Subtle elevation translation on service cards (`-4px`), gentle pulsating glow on WhatsApp floating trigger, responsive mobile drawers.
